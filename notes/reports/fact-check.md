**WRONG:** none of the 15 claims. Three need rewording before they go out:
- **#2:** the repo now holds 719 manuscripts, not 722. Three were withdrawn on 7 Oct, and the 42% figure is 300/719, measured after that.
- **#3:** the money funds workshops on evaluating AI results, new tools and community standards, not only checking.
- **#11:** the site is co-maintained by Davis, Ivanisvili and Tao, not Tao alone.
